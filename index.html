About the Guild

A haven for those who search for the ask and the answer.

This club is a haven for those who search for the ask and the answer. We welcome all those who wish to unravel the mysteries of the universe and build a community for curious minds with ambitious dreams.

We believe in encouraging questions, honoring every opinion and idea, and creating a space where students can discuss interests, spark inspiration, and connect through a shared love of space.

Our mission

To build a community for all space lovers.

What we do

Activities that turn wonder into experience.

Guest lectures & seminars

Talks from astronomers, astrophysicists, and aerospace enthusiasts from institutions such as IUCAA and beyond.

Creative projects

Solar system models, constellation dioramas, posters, and imaginative presentations inspired by space.

Space quiz nights

Friendly competition, curiosity, and a fun way to grow knowledge with every round.

Outreach & workshops

Astronomy-based community talks and sessions designed to inspire younger learners.
